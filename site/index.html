How it works

  • Move a player: drag its body. It slides along its own slot, as on the real table.
  • Turn a player: drag its stick, or drag the yellow ring that appears when the player is selected. Hold Shift for steps of 5 degrees. The mouse wheel over a selected player and the arrow keys turn it too.
  • Puck: drag it. Near a stick it snaps to the blade.
  • Pass and Run arrows: pick the tool, tap the ice for each point, then Done (or double-tap, or Enter). Select an arrow to move its points, curve it or change its colour.
  • Numbers: pick Number and tap where each touch happens.
  • View: drag empty ice to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to pan.
  • Keys: V move, A pass, D run, N number, Del delete, Ctrl+Z undo, Space play.

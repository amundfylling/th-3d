How to read the replay

  • White/blue figures (left end) are blue, yellow figures (right end) are orange. The figures stay with the table; the players switch ends 2-2-1-1-1, so the title names who played which end in that game.
  • Each figure: a dot on its tracked pivot, an arrow for where it faces, a short stick to the blade. A hollow dot is interpolated (the model reading was rejected or between 5-per-second readings). Figures the tracker lost are left out.
  • Puck (synthetic puck detector): black disk when it is slow (under 0.3 m/s), black ring when it moves faster. No puck means not seen in that frame. The grey line is its last half second.
  • Marked goals run from 8 s before to 1 s after your goal mark (red tick). The other goals have only the score-box time; the box changes 3 to 14 s after the goal (median s in your marks), so their window is the 17 s before the box and the goal is somewhere inside it.
  • Blue and centre lines are nominal; the goals are the preview cage. Game 4's overtime winner has no replay: the score box never shows it, so there is no time for it.
